# GROWTH app: what the screen recording shows

Source: Rob's screen recording of the app, 12 Oct 2026 (3 min 33 s, iPhone), read frame by frame, plus the App Store listing ("Growth - Gym & Fitness Planner", by Valentin Razvan Domnaru, v1.2.0). This is what was seen on screen, not a review of how well it works. Frames were sampled every 3 seconds, so anything between samples was not seen. The meal logger and a full workout were not exercised in the recording.

## Structure
Five tabs: **Progress · Workouts · Home · Meal Tracker · Profile**. Red on near-black, a glowing wordmark across the top, skeleton placeholder cards while a screen loads.

## 1. Onboarding (about 20 steps, one question per screen, progress bar, back arrow)
1. Name.
2. Experience: Beginner (under 1 year), Intermediate (1-2), Advanced (2+).
3. Where you train: Bodyweight only, Little equipment (dumbbells, bands), Home gym (rack, barbell, plates), Commercial gym.
4. Activity level: Not / Lightly / Moderately / Very active. Picking one shows what it means ("3-4 workouts a week, 4000-7000 steps a day").
5. Tried other fitness apps? Then a "results that last" chart (with GROWTH vs without).
6. Height and weight on scroll wheels, Imperial/Metric switch.
7. Birth date on scroll wheels.
8. Goal: Lose weight / Maintain / Gain weight.
9. What stops you (multi-select): consistency, unhealthy eating, lack of support, busy schedule, lack of meal inspiration.
10. What would you like to accomplish: eat healthier, energy and mood, stay motivated, feel better about my body.
11. Two selling screens ("Train with purpose", "Log meals effortlessly" with a phone demo).
12. A "We're setting everything up for you" screen counting 0-100% with a checklist ticking off Calories, Carbs, Protein, Fats, Workout Split, Goal duration.
13. **"Your custom plan is ready"**: daily calories (2073) in a ring, protein/carbs/fat in rings, an edit pencil, a recommended workout plan with a photo (Upper Lower 4/7 days, Intermediate), "Your stats" (weight, activity, equipment, target), "How to reach your goals", an FAQ accordion.
14. Create account (Apple, Google, Email), "try free, no payment due now", paywall (1 year $169.99, 1 month $22.99, Redeem Code, Restore Purchases), "Setting up your account", "You're all set".
15. A 10-step tour of the app (step 2: "Log meals easily: type what you ate in plain language").

## 2. Home
- Next Workout card (level and name, e.g. Intermediate / Upper Body).
- Weight chart.
- Streak flame animation with the week's days as dots.

## 3. Meal Tracker (chat style)
- A chat thread: "Let's track your meals. Be as explicit as possible!" with a text box "Log your meals...", a + button and a microphone. The marketing demo shows replies like "Logged: Chicken Breast, 412 kcal, protein 77.5 g".
- A pull-up sheet **Today's Macros**: calories eaten against the goal in a ring (0 / 2073), then protein, carbs and fat each in their own coloured ring with an icon. "View more nutrients" expands it. "Edit" opens **Edit Macro Goals** (calories, protein, carbs, fat, "Restore Default").
- **Recent Meals** with a calendar button.
- The store listing adds: photo logging, voice logging, plain-language typing, barcode scan (4M+ products).

## 4. Workouts
- **Recommended plans** grouped ("Full-Body Splits"): Upper Lower 4/7 Days, Bro Split, Upper Lower Push Pull Legs. Each shows "best suited for Intermediate/Advanced", a favourite star and View. **My Workouts** and **Filters** buttons.
- A workout page: exercise list with a photo each ("Exercise 3 of 7"), **Add Exercise**, **Start Workout**.
- An exercise page: demo video (play button), written instructions, **History** and **Replace** buttons, "Recommended number of sets: 3. Aim for 8-10 repetitions per set." Set rows show reps, set number, weight (kg), pre-filled with suggestions. **Remove Set / Add Set**. **Rest Duration 60 s** with Start. **Complete Exercise**.
- **Custom Workouts**: Create Workout, a name pre-filled "Custom Workout, October 12", a search box for exercises, sort by Alphabetical or Muscle Group, a photo thumbnail beside each exercise.
- The listing: 300+ exercises with demo videos from multiple angles, instructions, targeted muscles and equipment.

## 5. Progress
- Weight progress chart with All Time / Current.
- **Adjust Goal**: starting weight, goal weight, speed (Slow / Normal "Recommended" / Fast / Aggressive "Not recommended", with the weekly kg shown), activity level.
- KG LOST and KG GAINED tiles.
- **Weekly Check-in**: week number, weight, date, three photo slots (front, back, side).
- The listing: weekly check-ins automatically update calories and macros.

## 6. Profile
Avatar, name, a tagline ("Absolute Legend"), Current Streak / Goals Achieved / Workouts, a Mon-Sun tick strip, Edit Profile (email, birth date, gender, height), Settings.

## What Arete lacks compared with this (for deciding what to build)
1. A guided setup that asks experience, equipment, activity and goal speed and then builds calories, macros and a workout split in one go, with a "setting everything up" moment.
2. Prebuilt programs labelled by experience and equipment, with Filters and favourites.
3. Exercise demo videos and photos. Arete has a form cue only.
4. A "recommended sets and reps" line per exercise. Arete shows the rep range and a suggested weight, not the number of sets.
5. Goal speed presets (Slow / Normal / Fast / Aggressive) with the weekly kg and a "not recommended" flag.
6. A one-screen weekly check-in (weight, date, three photos).
7. A guided tour of the app on first use.
8. Chat-style logging in plain language as the main way to log a meal, and macro rings in a pull-up sheet. Arete has Describe a meal and macro bars.
9. Skeleton placeholder cards while screens load.
10. A streak flame with the week strip, and a "goals achieved" counter.

Arete already has, and GROWTH does not show: training load and readiness, sleep and recovery, skin routine and photo analysis, water coach, supplies forecasting, finance, notes, a muscle recovery map, a logging calendar, an exercise database with search, and your own split builder.
